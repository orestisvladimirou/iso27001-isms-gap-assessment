## Finding structure

- Requirement and precise evidence anchor.
- Objective nonconformity or observation with scope and limitation.
- Responsible owner and due date.
- Root cause, corrective and preventive action.
- Follow-up effectiveness test and final disposition.
- No invented signatures; distinguish rehearsal from actual audit.

**Note:** Actual nonconformity classification requires trained auditor judgment and relevant organizational audit rules.
