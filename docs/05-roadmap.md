# 05 — Prioritized 30/60/90-day governance roadmap

Dates below are **scenario milestones**, not promises of work for a real company.

| Window | Governance deliverable | Acceptance evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 0–30 | Sponsor, management security policy, risk appetite and ISMS boundary approved | Signed decisions, issue/change history, named owners |
| Days 0–30 | Full privileged identity inventory and MFA enforcement | 7/7 fictional admin identities accounted for, test of exceptions |
| Days 0–30 | Critical cloud hardening and vulnerability queue reviewed | Baseline export, closed findings or approved time-bound exceptions |
| Days 30–60 | Complete asset mapping/classification and data flow diagram | In-scope assets/stores have signed owners |
| Days 30–60 | Working incident response playbooks and communication paths | Tabletop results, lessons/actions, approved escalation tree |
| Days 30–60 | Restore/exit and operational resilience exercise | Business-defined RTO/RPO goals met; remediation of failures |
| Days 60–90 | Security objectives, metrics and monitoring review | Monthly dashboard, assigned metric owners, evidence of alerts |
| Days 60–90 | Finalize SoA decisions for remaining 70 unreviewed references | 93/93 addressed with explicit inclusion/exclusion rationale and implementation status |
| Days 60–90 | Independent internal audit and documented management review | Audit plan and sample findings; management review minutes; tracked corrective actions |

A true ISMS may require considerably longer, extra skills and external legal or auditing help. **This is a planning exercise, not a prediction of certification timelines.**

## Example objectives to operationalize

- Administrative MFA: target 100% of relevant in-scope human privileged identities, with protected emergency access.
- Information inventory: target 100% of discovered in-scope information stores with named owners and classifications.
- Logging coverage: target nine of nine modeled priority source classes, with alert-quality review.
- Training: target all 30 staff or justified documented exceptions; measure reporting behavior, not attendance alone.
- Tested recovery: target actual service owner-approved restoration objectives, measured in an independently witnessed scenario.

## Future development phases

**Phase 2:** Complete applicable/necessary-control justification across all 93 references, introduce explicit exclusions if genuinely justified, and link each control to risks and treatment decisions. Add a policy approval and document-control register.

**Phase 3:** Conduct a mock internal audit against Clause 4–10 evidence, write simulated nonconformities with root cause/corrective actions, and perform a management-review exercise. Contrast findings before and after fictional remediation evidence.

The baseline remains immutable for auditability; new evidence should be recorded in versioned follow-up data rather than silently overwriting original judgments.
