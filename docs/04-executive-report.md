# 04 — Fictional executive ISMS gap report

**Company:** Selene Payments Ltd (**fictional**)  
**Assessment date:** 2026-11-05  
**To:** Proposed Managing Director / Security Steering Group  
**From:** Independent project assessor, in a simulated exercise

> **Not an ISO certification readiness opinion or a real internal audit.** All evidence and business systems are fabricated.

## Executive assessment

This sample program has **not demonstrated a functioning ISO/IEC 27001 management system**. The most urgent weaknesses concern management sponsorship, privileged access, asset identification, vulnerability management, cloud configuration and recovery validation. A formally approved security policy, risk ownership model, audit programme and management review are missing.

**Risk register:** 10 modeled scenarios, including **2 Critical**, **6 High** and **2 Medium** residual risks. None have a documented real risk-acceptance decision.

**Clause checkpoint review:** 24 selected ISMS topics: **14 Gap**, **9 Partial**, **1 Not assessed**. These are case-study findings, not definitive audit nonconformities.

**Annex A:** All 93 control references exist in a **provisional** SoA. Only 23 have detailed gap observations. The rest require review before a full applicability statement could be claimed.

## Management decisions requested (fictional proposal)

1. Approve an ISMS sponsor and responsible risk-acceptance authority, confirming business boundaries and accountable owners.
2. Prioritize critical risk treatment, particularly privileged identity, cloud hardening, vulnerability and restoration evidence.
3. Commission a **genuine** internal audit programme only once draft policies and operating records are available, preserving independence.
4. Establish documented security objectives and a quarterly management review cadence.
5. Require closure evidence and reassess risks; do not call the organization compliant simply because the spreadsheet is populated.

## Evidence confidence and limitations

The evidence file set includes missing items and deliberately synthetic records. Only the laptop encryption scenario credits limited, fictional operational evidence. **The scores and statuses are training judgments, not assurance about a real organization.** Regulatory obligations depend on actual licensing, customer data processing and service scope.
