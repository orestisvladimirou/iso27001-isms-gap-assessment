- **Prior actions:** No previous signed management review exists in the baseline, so historical action approval and closure cannot be verified.
- **Internal/external context and interested parties:** Payments API availability, employee endpoints and cloud dependencies; scope and regulatory duties remain scenario assumptions. The 2024 climate-action amendment prompts consideration of whether climate-related issues affect this fictional ISMS context. No affirmative compliance claim follows.
- **Audit/review feedback:** Nine indicative findings (six Major-style, three Minor-style) from a non-independent real-world standpoint; audit programme not formally approved.
- **Risk assessment and treatment:** Initial ten residual risks had two Critical, six High, two Medium. In the later **hypothetical** follow-up, ISMS-R01 improves from 20 Critical to 10 High after scoped MFA evidence, leaving one Critical, seven High, two Medium. None are accepted by real management.
- **Monitoring and security objectives:** 7/7 hypothetical admin MFA, 30/30 staff attendance (learning unverified), only 5/9 log-source coverage, six overdue critical vulnerabilities, and zero complete recovery exercises.
- **Changes and opportunities:** Need to validate both draft SoA exclusions, define an ongoing audit cadence and strengthen training-effectiveness checks.
