# EV-007 — Endpoint encryption sample

**SYNTHETIC TEST OBSERVATION — DO NOT CLAIM A REAL CONTROL TEST.**

The scenario contains 15 managed laptops. An invented MDM export covers all 15 and reports full-disk encryption enabled; two representative test devices are notionally checked for recovery-key escrow and lock state. No real users, serial numbers, device IDs or secrets are used.

**Assessor note:** Within this hypothetical scope, the test supports lowering likelihood of data disclosure following device loss from 4 to 2, while impact remains 3. It does not establish the entire organization's cryptographic compliance.
