# EV-001 — Draft ISMS policy and governance roles

**SYNTHETIC EVIDENCE — NO REAL COMPANY OR SIGNATURE.**

The fictional security team maintains a draft security-policy document identifying information protection as a business objective. It lists CIO, IT Security Manager and system owners; no named management approval, issued version or communication record is present. It does not contain an approved ISMS risk-acceptance RACI.

**Assessor note:** A draft is a governance starting point but does not establish effective leadership approval.
