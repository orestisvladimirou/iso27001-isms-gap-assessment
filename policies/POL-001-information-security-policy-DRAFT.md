## Purpose and scope

This proposed policy explains how the fictional business would protect its information throughout the defined ISMS boundary. It does **not** mean controls described here are implemented.

## Principles

1. Classify information according to business sensitivity, assign accountable owners and restrict access by need and role.
2. Use multifactor authentication for privileged access and identify exceptions with compensating safeguards and accountable approval.
3. Monitor and correct material vulnerabilities and configuration drift through a measured lifecycle.
4. Preserve operational logs and report security events promptly through approved escalation procedures.
5. Back up critical information and test restorations against agreed business objectives.
6. Train personnel and require them to report suspicious activity and potential information-security events.
7. Review information security risks during significant service changes and at defined intervals.
8. Record management decisions, evidence, internal audits, corrective actions and improvement activities.

## Responsibilities

The Managing Director would approve the ISMS policy and resources. The proposed Security Manager would maintain procedures and communicate objectives; designated asset owners would accept treatment decisions within defined authority; Internal Audit would independently review the programme.

## Exceptions and review

Security exceptions would require documented rationale, compensating controls, expiry and accountable approval. This proposal would be reviewed at least annually or following material changes.
